According to recent reports, teams that ship generative AI to production know that a model is only as good as its data. That is why preparing high-quality training and evaluation data comes first. Fine-tuning an open-weight model, running reinforcement learning, or building an evaluation suite all depend on data that is accurate, well-labeled, and representative of your task. For narrow, well-defined tasks, a smaller model customized on clean, task-specific data can often match or exceed the accuracy of a larger general-purpose model prompted into shape, at a lower cost per inference.

In practice, though, preparing that data is often the slowest part of the project. Labeled examples are scarce or expensive, and privacy rules limit what you can use. Quality problems such as duplicates, label noise, and answer leakage go unnoticed until a training run disappoints, and every training technique expects data in its own format. As a result, data scientists spend weeks wrangling data instead of building models.

It has been revealed that today, we’re expanding Amazon SageMaker AI with upcoming AI data preparation capabilities for model development workflows. You can generate synthetic examples, assess and improve existing datasets, and bring expert feedback into the process through Python SDK APIs and the AWS AI-ML agent skill. You work in your notebook or IDE, with specifications and code that you can inspect and change.

With AI Data Preparation capabilities, you don’t need to stitch together labeling tools, generation scripts, and validation jobs. Each capability is a standalone building block. You can use one of them on its own or chain them together according to the workflow you want.

According to recent reports, you work wherever you already work. Use the SageMaker Python SDK directly or provide your coding agent the AI-ML skill in the agent toolkit and let your agent invoke the APIs according to established data science best practices. Describe what you need, and the agent selects and runs the right capability in SageMaker Studio JupyterLab, Kiro, or your local IDE with your coding assistant of choice.

For teams building specialized models, the hardest part is often finding enough task-specific data. Splunk, a Cisco company, faced exactly this when building evaluation models for its Agent Observability product.

Industry observers note that “For Splunk Agent Observability, our ability to train robust GenAI Eval models relies on access to high-quality, diverse datasets which are hard to curate for very specific tasks. Amazon SageMaker AI data preparation capabilities for synthetic data generation is meaningfully helping us scale these capabilities. We’ve been able to generate statistically representative synthetic datasets reliably and fast, while preserving the underlying patterns our models depend on. The seamless integration with the rest of our SageMaker workflow means our data scientists and customers spend less time wrangling data and more time building models that drive real enterprise value through Splunk.”.

— Yash Sheth, Director of Engineering, Splunk Agent Observability.
