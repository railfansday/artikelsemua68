Meta ads is now available in AWS Quick

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Marketers have never had more data to act on. Take an apparel brand that advertises on Meta technologies: campaign results live in Meta Ads Manager, briefs and creative assets in shared storage, and inventory, customer lifetime value, and in-store purchases in a data lake. Analysts export reports, merge spreadsheets, and rebuild the same deck each week, and the data ages before anyone acts on it. When the team does decide what to change, someone returns to Ads Manager to upgrade campaigns, ad sets, budgets, and audiences by hand. As a result, teams judge campaigns on metrics alone, and the path from insight to action stays slow and manual.

In a recent development, today, AWS Quick added a Meta ads AI connector. The connector gives Quick access to ads Model Context Protocol (MCP) server operations. Meta ads customers can use natural language to create, manage, and analyze campaigns within Quick, while staying connected to the Meta ad management network. Marketers can complete tasks with support from AI, such as pulling insights to understand ads performance, creating product catalogs, and editing existing or creating new campaigns. Quick can also combine Meta ad management data with first- and third-party data sources to create dashboards, documents, slide decks, and automated workflows.

Recent findings suggest that “Meta ads AI connector through Quick gives our marketing teams one place to go from campaign planning to execution. It lets us act on business insights directly in our ads, without the handoffs and tool switching that slow campaigns down. With fal.ai’s image, audio, and video models in the same workflow, we’ll test more creative ideas and spend more time reaching customers.”.

— Tina Sang, Head of Marketing at fal.

In a recent development, the video shows how to install the Meta ads connector from Customize > Connectors in AWS Quick, sign in with Facebook, and choose which Pages, Businesses, and Instagram accounts AWS Quick can access. It then runs two example prompts. One turns past campaign results into an eight-slide launch-initiative deck. The other builds a dashboard comparing spend, leads, and cost per lead for two ad sets, with recommended tests to run next.
