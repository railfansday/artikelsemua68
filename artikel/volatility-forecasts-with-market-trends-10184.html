Volatility Forecasts with Market Trends

🌐 Source: Arxiv • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (123 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

arXiv:2606.20145v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We show how volatility forecasts for financial markets can be refined by accounting for the current trends in these markets. Empirically, volatilities tend to increase day after day in times of strong up- or down-trends. This effect is particularly pronounced during down-trends. We find that it can accurately be modeled by including quadratic polynomials of the current trend strength as explanatory factors. Such factors can improve the predictive power of a wide range of volatility models, ranging from linear EWMA-based models over GARCH models and range-based models to stochastic volatility models. Our results are based on a robust statistical analysis of three decades of daily futures prices across equity-, bond-, FX- and commodity markets.
