Turn one person’s process into your team’s process, get more done in the Quick web app, and get more complete answers from agents working together.

AWS Quick is the AI workspace built only for work. Today, we are releasing updates that help your team get more of its everyday work done:.

Industry observers note that AI assistants make individuals faster, but the gains often stay with one person. The way one analyst builds the monthly report rarely becomes the way the team builds it, and work waits until someone is back at their desk. This release helps your team turn what one person knows into a process everyone can rely on, and keeps work moving when you step away.

Industry observers note that AWS Quick Flows already helps teams automate and standardize business processes so people can focus on their most important work. Now you can build, run, and refine flows entirely in chat. And your flows can take on more of your tough work, whether you start it from the web, desktop, or mobile app.

Recent findings suggest that for example, here is how flows could support a finance team. Maya is a financial planning and analysis manager at a retailer. Each month, she prepares a report for her VP of Finance that explains changes to costs and margins. For each region, she pulls last month’s results, compares them to the plan in a spreadsheet, writes commentary on the biggest changes, and sends the report. The process lives in her memory and a personal checklist. When she is out, a teammate has to rebuild the report from scratch, and it looks different every time.

With Quick, Maya can standardize the process, automate it, and make it available to her team. She describes her process in chat, the way she would explain it to a new employee. Quick builds the flow from her description and she tests and refines it in the same conversation. A diagram beside the chat demonstrates each step, so she can confirm it matches how she works.

Sources indicate that if you already use Quick in your browser, the updated web app puts the items that need your attention on the home screen as soon as you sign in. A reorganized left navigation brings agents, flows, skills, knowledge, and connectors together under Customize. It includes the agents and skills you built on desktop and ones others have shared with you. Your existing dashboards, topics, and spaces work as before, and you do not need to turn anything on.

In a recent development, some requests need the knowledge of more than one specialized agent. With multi-agent orchestration, the agent you ask can bring in several specialized agents at once, each working on the part it knows best. The agent then combines their work into one response. Each agent keeps its own instructions, knowledge, and context, so the people who know that work best can build and maintain it.

In a recent development, for example, you might ask why shipping costs rose in one region. Your agent can bring in a forecasting agent built on your team’s planning data and a procurement agent built on the procurement team’s vendor contracts. You get one response that demonstrates what each agent contributed, and you decide what to do with it.
